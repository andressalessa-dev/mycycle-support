# MyCycle Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

MyCycle is designed to help users track their menstrual cycle, symptoms, flow, and personal notes while keeping privacy in mind.

## Information Stored in the App

MyCycle may allow you to enter information such as:

- Menstrual cycle dates
- Flow intensity
- Symptoms
- Personal notes

This information is stored for the purpose of providing the app's core tracking functionality.

## Analytics

MyCycle may use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to help improve its functionality, performance, and user experience.

Analytics data may include information such as:

- App launches
- Screen views
- Feature usage
- General device and app information
- Crash and performance information, where applicable

MyCycle does not use analytics with the intention of identifying individual users or reading the personal cycle notes they enter into the app.

## Personal Health Data

Information you enter about your menstrual cycle, symptoms, flow, and notes is not intended to be used for advertising purposes.

## Third-Party Services

MyCycle may use services provided by Google Firebase for analytics and app performance purposes.

These services may process technical and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## Data Sharing

MyCycle does not sell personal data.

We do not share users' menstrual cycle entries or personal notes with advertisers.

## Children

MyCycle is not specifically directed at children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as MyCycle evolves. Any changes will be published on this page.
